# Ghana School of Law Campus Maps


## Project Overview

Ghana School of Law Campus Maps is a web-based wayfinding platform for the five Ghana School of Law campuses. It provides interactive campus maps that help students, staff, and visitors identify lecture venues, administrative offices, and essential services before or during a visit.

## Problem Statement

Students, staff, and visitors must navigate five Ghana School of Law campuses, each with a different layout and arrangement of facilities. Existing navigation tools primarily help users reach a campus but provide limited information about internal destinations such as lecture halls, libraries, examination venues, administrative offices, clinics, canteens, and washrooms.

As a result, users may depend on verbal directions or fragmented information, increasing the time and effort required to find their destinations. The Ghana School of Law therefore needs a reliable and centralized source of campus-level wayfinding information.

## Project Goal

To develop a centralized, accessible, and mobile-responsive campus wayfinding platform that enables students, staff, and visitors to quickly and accurately locate buildings, lecture venues, services, and other essential facilities across all five Ghana School of Law campuses.

## Project Objectives

The project will:

1. Provide a single directory through which users can select any Ghana School of Law campus.
2. Present clear, interactive maps showing important locations within each campus.
3. Allow users to find lecture venues, administrative offices, and essential services with minimal effort.
4. Provide useful information about selected locations, including rooms, facilities, and links to external geographic coordinates where available.
5. Deliver a responsive and accessible experience across supported mobile and desktop browsers.
6. Maintain a consistent data structure that makes campus and location information easier to update or extend.

## Primary Users

- Current and prospective students
- Academic and administrative staff
- Visitors, guests, and service providers
- Project administrators responsible for maintaining campus information

## Scope

### In Scope

- A directory of all five Ghana School of Law campuses
- Interactive visual maps for each campus
- Identification of buildings, rooms, lecture venues, and essential services
- Location details and Google Maps links where coordinates are available
- Search or selection of mapped locations
- Responsive support for mobile and desktop devices

### Out of Scope

- Real-time indoor positioning or turn-by-turn navigation
- Live traffic, occupancy, or room-availability information
- A native Android or iOS application
- An administrative content-management system

## Expected Outcomes

The project is expected to:

- Reduce the time users spend searching for campus locations.
- Decrease reliance on verbal directions and fragmented location information.
- Improve access to accurate and consistent campus information.
- Provide a convenient navigation experience across mobile and desktop devices.
- Improve the overall campus experience for students, staff, and visitors.

## Proposed Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- Users can access all five campus maps from a single directory.
- Users can locate common destinations and view relevant details without external assistance.
- Interactive map controls work consistently across the supported browsers and screen sizes.
- Campus and location information can be updated without redesigning the entire platform.
- Usability testing shows that users can complete common location-finding tasks accurately and efficiently.

Specific targets for task-completion rate, average completion time, and user-satisfaction score should be agreed with the tech lead before formal usability testing.

## Technical Approach

The platform is implemented as a lightweight static website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It requires no backend service, external dependency, or build process, which keeps deployment and maintenance straightforward.
